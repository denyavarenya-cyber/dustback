DustBack

Your wallet. Your value.
Back where it belongs.

Every Solana wallet slowly collects leftovers: empty token accounts that still hold a small SOL deposit, and dust balances too small to trade by hand. DustBack scans any wallet, shows exactly what can be recovered, closes the empty accounts and swaps the dust to SOL — in one reviewed, signed sweep.

  • Scan without connecting — a public address is enough.
  • Every account, swap and fee is shown before you sign.
  • The fee is only charged on value actually delivered.
  • No approvals, no delegates, no permissions — ever.
  • Open source, so you can check all of the above.
