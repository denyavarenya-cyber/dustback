DustBack is now part of Noventia.

Wallet cleanup lives in the Noventia app.

DustBack closed empty token accounts on Solana and returned their rent to the owner. That work continues in Noventia, a record of what wallets, projects and agents do on Solana, built from facts anyone can check.

The DustBack Android app is no longer distributed. DustBack never held funds: every transaction was signed in the owner's own wallet.
