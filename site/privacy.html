Privacy policy

Effective 2 September 2026

DustBack has no accounts, no sign-up and no backend of its own. We do not collect, store or sell any personal data. The app ships without analytics or tracking SDKs beyond what the app store platform itself provides.

What the app sends, and to whom

Everything DustBack does happens on your device. To perform the scan or sweep you request, the app sends the wallet address you entered (or connected) to a Solana RPC endpoint, and token amounts and mints to Jupiter's public APIs to fetch prices, quotes and swap transactions. These requests are the mechanics of the service you asked for — nothing is sent anywhere else, and we run no server that could see or keep any of it.

What we never touch

Your seed phrase and private keys never reach DustBack. Signing happens inside your own wallet app via the Mobile Wallet Adapter. DustBack never requests token approvals or delegates.
